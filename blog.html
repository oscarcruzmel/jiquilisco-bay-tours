Why this place matters

Jiquilisco Bay is El Salvador's largest brackish-water and saltwater-forest complex and has been a Ramsar Wetland of International Importance since 2005. These guides use official tourism and wetland-conservation sources while connecting visitors with practical ways to experience the region.
