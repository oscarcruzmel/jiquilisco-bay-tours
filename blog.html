DISCOVER JIQUILISCO BAY

More than a destination. A landscape shaped by water, mangroves and coastal communities.

Bahía de Jiquilisco lies on El Salvador’s southeastern Pacific coast in Usulután. Its network of estuaries, mangrove channels, beaches, sandbars, islands and lagoons forms the country’s largest brackish-water and mangrove wetland complex. The area was designated a Wetland of International Importance under the Ramsar Convention in 2005, and the wider Xiriualtique–Jiquilisco area became a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve in 2007.

What makes Jiquilisco Bay special?

The bay contains El Salvador’s largest mangrove expanse and provides habitat for an extraordinary variety of coastal wildlife. Mangroves, waterways, beaches and islands create a landscape best experienced slowly—from the water.

PLACES TO SEE

Islands, mangroves and coastal stops

Places associated with the bay include Isla Espíritu Santo, Isla Méndez, La Pirraya, San Sebastián, Puerto El Triunfo, Puerto Parada and the Punta San Juan del Gozo area. Each offers a different perspective on the bay’s water, beaches, coconut palms, communities and mangrove environment.

THINGS TO DO

Build a day around the water

Boat excursions are one of the best ways to experience the bay. Other activities promoted in the region include birdwatching, fishing, kayaking, swimming and relaxing along beaches and sandbars. Sunsets are one of the bay’s signature experiences.

HISTORY & CONSERVATION

A wetland recognized internationally

The Complejo Bahía de Jiquilisco was designated Ramsar Site No. 1586 on October 31, 2005. UNESCO nominated the Xiriualtique–Jiquilisco Biosphere Reserve in 2007. The ecosystem supports local livelihoods including artisan fishing while protecting biodiversity and helping stabilize the coast.

VISITOR IDEA

Begin at the coast, then explore deeper.

Puerto El Triunfo and Puerto Parada are established gateways for excursions into the bay. From the water, visitors can experience mangrove channels, islands, birdlife and views toward the Pacific coast. For a more remote coastal experience, Punta San Juan del Gozo adds beach scenery and a very different side of the region.
