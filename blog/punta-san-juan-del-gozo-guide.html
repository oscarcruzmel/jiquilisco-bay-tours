Guide to Punta San Juan del Gozo

A remote peninsula where the Jiquilisco Bay landscape meets El Salvador's Pacific coast.

Where is Punta San Juan del Gozo?

San Juan del Gozo is part of the coastal landscape associated with Jiquilisco Bay in Usulután. El Salvador's Ministry of Tourism describes the bay as extending along the San Juan del Gozo peninsula, with the Río Grande de San Miguel reaching the area and connections from the bay toward the Pacific.

What makes it different?

This is not a highly developed resort strip. Its appeal is the combination of open Pacific shoreline, tropical coastal scenery and proximity to the mangrove-and-estuary environment of Jiquilisco Bay. Visitors should arrive expecting a remote, naturally beautiful and comparatively rustic destination.

Things to do

Spend time on the Pacific beach

The long shoreline is ideal for taking in the ocean landscape, walking and enjoying a quieter coastal setting.

Combine the beach with a bay tour

A boat trip adds the mangrove and estuary side of the destination, making it possible to experience two very different coastal environments in one visit.

Add an adventure activity

Jiquilisco Bay Tours can coordinate ATV beach riding and horseback riding subject to availability and conditions. These are add-ons rather than guaranteed daily activities.

Enjoy local food

A typical Salvadoran breakfast, lunch or dinner can be coordinated subject to local availability. Guests should disclose allergies and dietary restrictions in advance.

What to know before you go

Because the area is remote and still developing, roads, facilities, mobile coverage and services may not resemble established tourism centers. Bring sun protection, drinking water, suitable footwear and protection for electronics. Plans may need to change with weather, tides and local conditions.

Best for: travelers who value nature, space and authentic coastal surroundings more than polished resort infrastructure.
