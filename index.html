BEFORE YOU VISIT

Authentic, remote and still developing.

Punta San Juan del Gozo is a remote coastal area and is not fully developed for mass tourism. Conditions can be rustic. We want guests to know what to expect so they can enjoy the natural setting comfortably and safely.
