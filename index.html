Remote-area & activity noticePunta San Juan del Gozo and parts of Jiquilisco Bay are remote, rural and still developing. Roads, docks, beaches, trails, bathrooms, shops, communications and medical services may be basic, limited, irregular or unavailable. Boat travel, ATVs, horseback riding, beaches and mangrove excursions involve inherent outdoor and transportation risks. Guests must follow operator safety instructions and disclose relevant dietary, accessibility or health concerns before participating.
