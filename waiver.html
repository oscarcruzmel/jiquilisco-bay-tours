JIQUILISCO BAY TOURS · EL SALVADOR

Informed Risk Acknowledgment & Participation Authorization

Please read this entire document before electronically signing. This form supports informed consent and risk communication. It does not replace insurance, permits, inspections, maintenance, training, first aid procedures, or professional legal advice.

1. Participant information

The participant will provide full name, date of birth, identification/passport information, telephone, email, emergency contact, selected activities and activity date when signing electronically.

2. Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. “Operator” means Jiquilisco Bay Tours and its owners, administrators, guides, captains, drivers, suppliers and collaborators, each only within the scope of duties legally applicable to them.

3. Destination conditions

Punta San Juan del Gozo and parts of Jiquilisco Bay are remote, rural and not fully developed. Roads, docks, beaches, trails, bathrooms, shops, communications and medical services may be basic, irregular, limited or unavailable. Conditions can change because of weather, tides, maintenance, access and local availability.

4. General risks

Tourism and outdoor activities involve inherent risks even when reasonable safety measures are used. Risks may include:

  • Falls, slips, impacts, cuts, sprains, fractures, drowning or other injury.
  • Uneven terrain, sand, mud, roots, rocks, unpaved roads and limited access.
  • Heat, sun, rain, wind, storms, insects, wildlife, vegetation and dehydration.
  • Tides, waves, currents, visibility and changing water conditions.
  • Delays in rescue, transportation, communication or medical attention because of the remote location.
  • Unpredictable animal behavior, equipment or vehicle malfunction/misuse, and actions of other participants.
  • Loss, damage or theft of personal property.

5. Horseback-riding risks

Horses can startle, stumble, kick, bite, buck, turn or react unexpectedly. Falls and serious injuries can occur. Participants agree to follow the guide’s instructions, use required safety equipment, avoid alcohol/drugs or impairing medication, avoid racing or unauthorized maneuvers, treat animals appropriately and disclose relevant fears, injuries, pregnancy or medical concerns.

6. ATV risks

Operating or riding an ATV can result in rollovers, collisions, falls, burns, serious injury or death. Participants agree to use required protective equipment, operate only authorized vehicles, follow the guide’s instructions, maintain a prudent speed, avoid racing/stunts/unauthorized routes and report any malfunction, damage or loss of control immediately.

7. Boat-tour risks

Navigation risks include waves, tides, currents, boarding/disembarking, wet surfaces, impacts, falling overboard, mechanical issues and route changes. Participants agree to use life jackets when instructed, remain seated when required and obey the captain. The captain may modify, delay or cancel a trip for safety, weather, tide, vessel-capacity or operating reasons.

8. Health and capacity

Participants should disclose relevant cardiac or respiratory conditions, epilepsy, recent injuries, pregnancy, severe allergies, mobility limitations or other conditions that may affect safe participation. Disclosure does not automatically exclude participation, but the Operator may recommend against participation, adapt an activity or request medical authorization when reasonably necessary for safety.

9. Safety instructions

The Operator may deny, suspend or end participation when a person disobeys safety instructions, behaves recklessly or aggressively, appears impaired by alcohol/drugs, refuses required equipment or presents a condition that reasonably makes participation unsafe.

10. Informed acceptance of inherent risks

After receiving available information and an opportunity to ask questions, the participant voluntarily accepts the ordinary inherent risks of the selected activity that cannot reasonably be eliminated. This acknowledgment does not waive non-waivable consumer rights or exclude or limit responsibility that Salvadoran law does not permit to be excluded or limited.

11. Participant conduct

The participant is responsible for consequences attributable to intentional, reckless conduct or conduct contrary to clear instructions, including unauthorized equipment use, reckless driving, animal mistreatment or deliberate damage.

12. Emergency medical attention

If an emergency prevents the participant from deciding, the participant authorizes reasonable basic first aid, contact with emergency services or authorities, coordination of transport to an available medical facility, and sharing relevant health information with medical personnel. Remote location may delay response. Medical, evacuation or transport costs are the participant’s responsibility unless applicable law provides otherwise.

13. Minors

For a minor participant, the parent or legal representative confirms authority to authorize participation, has read this document, has explained safety rules appropriately to the minor and accepts the duty to supervise when required.

14. Food, allergies and dietary needs

Breakfast, lunch or dinner may be coordinated subject to local availability. Participants must disclose allergies, intolerances, religious restrictions or dietary needs in writing before the activity. Trace exposure or cross-contamination cannot be guaranteed absent express written confirmation.

15. Personal belongings

Participants are responsible for protecting documents, money, phones, cameras and other belongings. Bring only what is necessary and use protection from water, sand and impacts.

16. Photos and promotional material

Photo/video promotional consent is optional and does not affect participation or price.

17. Legal scope

This document is intended to inform participants of activity conditions and foreseeable risks, record voluntary participation, establish reasonable safety rules and document specific authorizations. It does not attempt to eliminate obligations or responsibilities that cannot legally be excluded, or require consumers to waive rights that Salvadoran law treats as non-waivable.

18. Governing law and severability

This document is interpreted under the laws of the Republic of El Salvador. If a provision is invalid, abusive or unenforceable, it will be disregarded or applied only to the maximum extent permitted without affecting provisions that may legally remain in effect.

19. Final participant declaration

By signing or accepting electronically, the participant confirms that they read and understood this document, received information about the nature, location and remote conditions of the activity, had an opportunity to ask questions, provided accurate health/contact information, agrees to follow safety instructions and understands that this declaration does not eliminate protections recognized as non-waivable under Salvadoran law.

Electronic acceptance: checking the acceptance box, typing the participant’s legal name and submitting the electronic form constitutes the participant’s electronic acceptance, subject to applicable law.

Operator note: this customer-facing form should be reviewed by an attorney licensed in El Salvador before being relied upon as a final legal waiver.
